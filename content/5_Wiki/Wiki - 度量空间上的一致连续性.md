### Definition 1 一致连续

对度量空间子集 $E\subset X$ [[Wiki - 度量空间]]，A function f is uniformly continuous in E if 
$\forall \epsilon>0,\exists \delta>0 , \forall \space x_{1}, x_{2}\in E,d(x_{1},x_{2})<\delta,d(f(x_{1}),f(x_{2}))<\epsilon$。
