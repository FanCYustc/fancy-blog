## The 1 可逆线性映射的判定

```ad-theorem
title: Theorem 1

设$\mathscr{A}:U\to V$是线性映射，下面几个条件等价：

$$\begin{aligned}
(1)&\mathscr{A}是可逆同构映射\\\\ 
(2)&~\dim(U)=\dim(V)~\text{且}~\ker(\mathscr{A})=0.\\\\(3)&~\dim(U)=\dim(V)~\text{且}~\operatorname{Im}(\mathscr{A})=V.\\\\(4)&~\ker(\mathscr{A})=0~\text{且}\operatorname{Im}(\mathscr{A})=V.\end{aligned}$$



```
