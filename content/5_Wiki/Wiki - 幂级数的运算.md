## 幂级数的加法

                     $$\begin{array}{*{20}{c}}{\mathop \sum \limits_{n = 0}^\infty  {a_n}{x^n} \pm \mathop \sum \limits_{n = 0}^\infty  {b_n}{x^n} = \mathop \sum \limits_{n = 0}^\infty  \left( {{a_n} \pm {b_n}} \right){x^n}}\\{R = {\rm{min}}\left\{ {{R_a},{R_b}} \right\}}\end{array}$$

## 幂级数的乘法
					 $$\begin{array}{*{20}{c}}{\mathop \sum \limits_{n = 0}^\infty  {a_n}{x^n}\cdot\mathop \sum \limits_{n = 0}^\infty  {b_n}{x^n} = \mathop \sum \limits_{n = 0}^\infty  \mathop \sum \limits_{k = 0}^n {a_k}{b_{n - k}}{x^n} = \mathop \sum \limits_{n = 0}^\infty  {c_n}{x^n}}\\{R = {\rm{min}}\left\{ {{R_a},{R_b}} \right\}}\end{array}$$
