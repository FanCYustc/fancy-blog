## Def 1 朗斯基行列式

```ad-definition
title: Definition 1

对齐次线性微分方程组 $\frac{d\boldsymbol{y}}{dx}=A(x)\boldsymbol{y}$

有 n 个解 $\boldsymbol{y}_{1}(x),\dots,\boldsymbol{y}_{n}(x)$,

将它们写分量形式，称

$$W(x)=\begin{vmatrix}y_{11}(x)&y_{12}(x)&\cdots&y_{1n}(x)\\y_{21}(x)&y_{22}(x)&\cdots&y_{2n}(x)\\...\\\\y_{n1}(x)&y_{n2}(x)&\cdots&y_{nn}(x)\end{vmatrix}$$
为**朗斯基行列式**

```
