## Def 1 brown 桥

```ad-definition
title: Definition 1


$\{Z (t), 0\leq t\leq1\}$ 称为布朗桥, 若其满足以下三条:
$$\begin{aligned}&(\mathrm{i})\quad Z(0)=Z(1)=0;\\&(\mathrm{ii})\quad\{Z(t),0\leq t\leq1\}\text{ 为高斯过程;}\\&(\mathrm{iii})\quad\text{对 }\forall0<s<t<1,\mathrm{E}Z(t)=0,\\&\mathrm{Cov}\left(Z(s),Z(t)\right)=s(1-t).\end{aligned}$$


```
