$R^1$ 的情形：

[[Wiki - 实数的完备性等价命题]]

#### Definition 1
K is **compact** if it can be covered by finite open sets.



#### Proposition 1
If K is compact, then
1. **K is closed in** $R^m$.
2. Any closed subset of K is compact.


#### Definition 2
The **diameter** of a set  $E \subset \mathbb{R}^{m}$  is

$d (E):=\sup _{x_{1}, x_{2} \in E} d\left (x_{1}, x_{2}\right)$ .


#### Definition 3
A set is bounded if its diameter is finite.


#### Proposition 2
K is compact $\to$ K is bounded in $\mathbb{R}^m$.


#### Proposition 3
K is compact $\leftrightarrow$ K is closed and bounded in $\mathbb{R}^m$.
