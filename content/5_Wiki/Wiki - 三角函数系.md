#数学分析/函数 

## Def1 三角函数系
$$
1,\cos x,\sin x,\ldots ,\cos nx,\sin nx,\ldots
$$

称为**三角函数系**

## Pro1 三角函数系的性质

$$
\begin{aligned}\int _{-\pi }^{\pi } 1\cdot \cos nxdx=\int _{-\pi }^{\pi } 1\cdot \sin nxdx=0\\

\dfrac{1}{\pi }\int _{-\pi }^{\pi }\cos mx\cdot \sin nxdx=0\left ( m, n\geq 1\right) \\

\dfrac{1}{\pi }\int _{-\pi }^{\pi }\cos mx\cdot \cos nxdx=\delta _{mn}\\

\dfrac{1}{\pi }\int _{-\pi }^{\pi }\sin mx\cdot \sin nxdx=\delta _{mn}\end{aligned}
$$
