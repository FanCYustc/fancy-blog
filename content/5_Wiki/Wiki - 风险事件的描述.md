## Def 1 彩票

```ad-definition
title: Definition 1

若一个事件对应多个结果，概率分别为 $\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{s}$，对应的结果分别为 $q_{1},q_{2},\dots,q_{s}$ [[Wiki - 效用]]

则 $Q=(\alpha;q)=(\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{s};q_{1},\dots,q_{s})$

称为一张彩票


```
