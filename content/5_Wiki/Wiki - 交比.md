## Def 1 交比

```ad-definition
title: Definition 1

互不相同的 $z_{1}, z_{2}, z_{3}, z_{4}\in \mathbb{C}_{\infty}$，定义交比

$$
(z_{1},z_{2},z_{3},z_{4})= \frac{{z_{1}-z_{3}}}{z_{1}-z_{4}} / \frac{{z_{2}-z_{3}}}{z_{2}-z_{4}}
$$
当 $z_{i}=\infty$ 时，包含 $z_{i}$ 的因子为 1

```
