## Def 1 一致最优检验

```ad-definition
title: Definition 1


$$\begin{aligned}&\text{Let }\Phi_\alpha\text{ denote all level }\alpha\text{ tests of testing problem }H_0:\theta\in\Theta_0\leftrightarrow H_1:\\&\theta\in\Theta_1.\text{ A test }\phi\in\Phi_\alpha\text{ is uniformly most powerful test (UMPT,一致}\\&\text{最优检验) if}\\&\beta_\phi(\theta)\geq\beta_{\phi_1}(\theta),\forall\phi_1\in\Phi_\alpha,\forall\theta\in\Theta_1\end{aligned}$$


```

即为限制在水平 $\alpha$ 上，使得犯二型错误的概率 $1-\beta(\theta)$ 最小 [[Wiki - 功效函数]]
