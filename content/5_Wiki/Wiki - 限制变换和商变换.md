#线性代数/线性变换 

[[Scientech - 线性代数-线性变换]] 

## Def 1 restriction operator

```ad-note
title: def 1

Suppose $T\in\mathcal{L}(V)$ and U is a subspace of V invariant under T[[Wiki - 不变子空间]]

The restriction operator $T|_{U}\in\mathcal{L}(U)$ is defined by

$$
T|_{U}(u)=Tu
$$

for $u\in U.$


```


## Def  2 quotient operator

```ad-definition
title: Definition 2

The quotient operator $T/U\in\mathcal{L}(V/U)$ is defined by

$$
(T/U)(\nu+U)=Tv+U
$$

for $\nu\in V.$

[[Wiki - 空间的商]]

```
