## The 1 riesz-fredholm 定理

$$\begin{aligned}&\text{设 }A\in\mathcal{C}(X),T=I-A\text{,则}\\&1.\dim(\mathrm{Ker}(T))<\infty.\\&2.\mathrm{~Ran}(T)\text{ 闭。}\\&3.\text{ Fredholm Alternative, 二择一律:}T\text{ 单}\Leftrightarrow T\text{ 满。}\\&4.\mathrm{~Ran}(T)=\mathrm{Ker}(T^*).\\&5.\dim(\mathrm{Ker}(T))=\dim(\mathrm{Ker}(T^*)).\end{aligned}$$
