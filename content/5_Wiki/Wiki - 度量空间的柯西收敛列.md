#### Definition 1

```ad-definition
title: Definition 1

$\{y_{k}\}, y_{k}\in \mathbb{R}^n$, is **a Cauchy sequence** if and only if $\forall \epsilon >0,\space\exists N\in\mathbb{N}\space s.t. d(y_{{k_{1}}},y_{{k_{2}}})<\epsilon$
For all $k_1,k_{2}>N$.

```
