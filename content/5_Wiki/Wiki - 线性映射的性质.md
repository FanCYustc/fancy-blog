#线性代数/线性变换 

[[Wiki - 线性映射]]
## Pro1 

```ad-proposition
title: Propositon 1

$$
\begin{array}{l}
A(0)=0 \\
A(-\alpha)=-A(\alpha) \\
\alpha_{1},\dots\alpha_{n} 是V的一组基，\alpha=\lambda_{1}\alpha_{1}+\dots+\lambda_{n}\alpha_{n}, \\
A(\alpha)=\lambda_{1}A(\alpha_{1})+\dots+\lambda_{n}A(\alpha_{n}) \\
\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{n}线性相关，A(\alpha_{1}),\dots,A(\alpha_{n})也线性相关
\end{array}
$$

```
