## Pro 1 保角性

```ad-proposition
title: Propositon 1

全纯函数在导数不为 0 的点保角

```

[[Wiki - 全纯函数]]

我们把这样保角的映射叫做**共形映射**[[Wiki - 共形映射]]

### Proof

过 $z_{0}$ 做光滑曲线 $\gamma (t), a\leq t\leq b$，$\gamma(a)=z_{0},f'(z_{0})\neq 0$

定义
$$\begin{aligned}w&=\sigma(t)=f(\gamma(t)),a\leqslant t\leqslant b.\\\sigma^{\prime}(a)&=f^{\prime}(\gamma(a))\gamma^{\prime}(a)=f^{\prime}(z_0)\gamma^{\prime}(a)\neq0\text{,}\end{aligned}$$

所以 $Arg \sigma' (a)-Arg \gamma'(a)=Arg f'(z_{0})$
