```ad-theorem
title: Theorem 1

$\textit{ Suppose B}= \{ B_1, B_2, \ldots , B_N\}$ is a finite collection of open balls in $\mathbb{R} ^d.$ Then there exists a disjoint sub- collection $B_{i_1}, B_{i_2}, \ldots , B_{i_k}$ $of$ $\mathcal{B}$ that satisfles
$$m\left(\bigcup_{\ell=1}^NB_\ell\right)\leq3^d\sum_{j=1}^km(B_{i_j}).$$



```
