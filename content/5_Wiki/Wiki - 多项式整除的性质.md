## Pro 1

1. $f\mid g,g\mid h\to f\mid h$
2. $f\mid g, g\mid f\to f=cg$，c 为常数
3. $f\mid g_{i}\to f\mid\sum a_{i}g_{i}$
4. Gcd (f, h)=1, f|gh->f|h [[Wiki - 多项式的互素]]
5. F|h, g|h, gcd (f, g)=1->fg|h
